The Silent Destruction

For too long, the lives of very real people have been treated as a political issue — debated by extremists on both the left and the right. Their shouting, their temper, and the mood of our leaders affect us, me. I cannot speak up without being seen as one of the extremists I reject. Anonymity is all I have left.

District Union

'District Union' reflects on a potential after the 2024 US Election future where the United States has become entirely self-centered and forgotten by the rest of the world. This piece follows Marion through her life and questioning of the system in the town of Branston Bow in Rural America which has been plagued by the fuel shortage and mass exodus of the population to the states' crime filled cities.

Light and Dark

Light and Dark is a piece promoting peace in the Israel-Palestine conflict, showing that we are all human whatever nationality, religion, or ideology you hold. It is also meant to show that crimes are still crimes in war. The piece follows a bus driving from the south coast on the Red Sea to the north in the Golan Heights in a fictional near-future of fragile peace.

2084

2084 is a dystopian parallel to the world-famous 1984 by George Orwell. This short story is a reflection of the 2024 United States Election's potential to change the future of America forever. In a future where Donald J Trump comes out victorious, he reshapes the idea of society to reward men loyal to him and his own ideology.
